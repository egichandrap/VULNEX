# ADR-010: AI Features Deferred Until After MVP

- Status: Accepted
- Decision: AI is not a dependency for MVP. Consider AI-assisted finding explanation and remediation after stable deterministic workflows exist.

## Context
The product must first establish trustworthy evidence, provenance, and normalized findings.

## Consequences
Post-MVP AI must cite source findings, distinguish evidence from inference, require human review, avoid autonomous exploitation, and protect tenant data. AI output must not silently change finding status or risk scores.
